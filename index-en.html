Ferienchalet Bödele Familie Feichtinger

Cozy holiday chalet at the Bödele, perfect for winter and summer holidays. Suitable for families, couples, or solo travelers. Pets are warmly welcome too.


Our Houses

Arrive. Unwind. Enjoy Bödele.

The Bödele offers a special mountain landscape in every season: in summer with pleasant, fresh mountain air and many hiking trails, in winter with snow, skiing, sledding, and beautiful walks through the snowy surroundings.

Our holiday houses form a small row-house complex between Schwarzenberg and Dornbirn.

The holiday houses offer a cozy living and dining area with kitchen, an entrance area, a balcony, and a bathroom with shower and toilet.

A staircase leads to the lower level with two bedrooms: one bedroom with a double bed and another with two single beds. A basement is also available, ideal for storing skis and other equipment.

The houses are easily reached by car, with parking available directly in front of them.

Vorarlberg is Austria’s westernmost state, bordering Germany, Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Schwarzenberg lies in the Vorderbregenzerwald region and is just a 5 to 10 minute drive away. Our holiday house is located between Schwarzenberg and Bödele at an altitude of 1,100 metres.

Activities & Excursions

Countless hiking trails offer beautiful views of the surrounding mountains throughout the year. The villages of the Bregenzerwald provide plenty of activities for nature lovers. In winter, you can enjoy skiing in the many ski areas across the region. Just a few minutes away, a ski school offers lessons for beginners, including a learn to ski guarantee. In summer, enjoy the fresh mountain air of the Vorarlberg Alps and explore the beautiful surroundings. Spend your holiday in the mountains while still being close to the city.
